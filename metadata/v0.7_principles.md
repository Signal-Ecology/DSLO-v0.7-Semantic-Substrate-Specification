**DSLO v0.7 Principles**

**August, 2026**  

**Discipline‑Level Meaning Systems (DSLO)**  

**v0.7 Public‑Layer Principles**

---
*DSLO v0.7 establishes the lawful architecture for* **substrate‑level meaning systems**. These **principles** *define the* **constraints**, **invariants**, **legality surfaces**, *and* **traversal rules** *governing the* **construction**, **propagation**, and **validation** of meaning across **substrates**, **manifolds**, **operators**, **runtime geometry**, and **supervisor geometry**. *They form the public‑layer foundation for scientific uptake, indexing, and cross‑discipline interoperability.*

---
1. **Substrate‑First Construction**
*All DSLO geometry originates in the substrate.*
Substrate surfaces must be **authored** or **substrate‑generated**, **never derived**.
*Generator traversal is only lawful after substrate completion*.

---
2. **Deterministic Inheritance**
*Every surface must inherit deterministically from its substrate roots.*
*No surface may introduce **drift**, **ambiguity**, or **non‑substrate logic**.
*Inheritance must preserve* **invariants**, **legality**, and **continuity**.

---
3. **Unified Substrate Manifold**
*All geometry exists within the Unified Substrate Manifold.*
**Derived manifolds** **(A, T, D, E, F, Ω)** *must project lawfully from the unified manifold*.
*Manifold transitions must preserve* **curvature**, **boundaries**, **invariants**, and **legality**.

---
4. **Lawful Geometry Propagation**
**Meaning**, **curvature**, **drift**, **load**, **legality**, and **identity** *must propagate lawfully*.
*Propagation must respect* **envelopes**, **masks**, **constraints**, and **stability modes**.
*Unlawful propagation constitutes collapse geometry*.

---
5. **Invariant Stability**
**Invariants govern substrate stability**, **manifold continuity**, **operator legality**, **relational geometry**, and **runtime behavior**. *Invariants must remain stable across* **transitions**, **updates**, 
and **drift events**.

---
7. **Legality as a First‑Class Geometry**
*Legality is geometric*.
**Legality masks**, **channels**, **constraints**, and **propagation surfaces** *must be authored or substrate‑generated*.
*All geometry must remain consistent with supervisor legality*.

---
8. **Runtime Meaning Geometry**
**Runtime geometry** **(DSUP)** *defines lawful meaning behavior under update cycles*.
*Runtime surfaces must be** **substrate‑generated**, **not authored or derived**.
*Runtime geometry must synchronize with** **manifold geometry** and **legality surfaces**.

---
9. **Generator‑Derived Surfaces**
*The generator may only produce derived surfaces*:
**ontology**, ***taxonomy**, **axioms**, **schema**, **relational geometry surfaces**, **legality geometry surfaces**, **manifold geometry surfaces**, **operator geometry surfaces**.
*Generator output must remain consistent with* **substrate invariants** and **legality**.

---
10. **Supervisor Geometry**
*Supervisor geometry enforces* **global legality**, **invariant stability**, **collapse prevention**, and **recovery behavior** *across all manifold layers*.
*Supervisor geometry must remain* **stable** and **non‑drifting**.

---
11. **Scientific Legibility**
*DSLO v0.7 must remain scientifically legible*.
*Minimal examples, diagrams, geometry walkthroughs, legality propagation examples, drift geometry examples, and supervisor coupling examples must be provided.*

---
**Summary**
*DSLO v0.7 Principles define the lawful architecture for substrate‑level meaning systems. They govern substrate construction, manifold geometry, legality propagation, runtime behavior, generator traversal, and scientific legibility. These principles ensure DSLO remains stable, deterministic, lawful, and scientifically accessible*.
