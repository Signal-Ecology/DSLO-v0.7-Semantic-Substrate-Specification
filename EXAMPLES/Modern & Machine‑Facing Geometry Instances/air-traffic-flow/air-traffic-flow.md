# Air Traffic Flow — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Air traffic flow is a global routing geometry shaped by altitude layers,
waypoints, airways, and wind fields. Aircraft follow constraint‑bounded paths
while ATC and automation systems maintain spacing and optimize flow.

**Key Properties**
- Altitude stratification  
- Flow‑field alignment  
- Waypoint routing  
- Dynamic spacing  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: propagation, partitioning, routing  
- Invariants: flow coherence, altitude stability  
- Manifolds: altitude, flow‑field, routing‑graph  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
