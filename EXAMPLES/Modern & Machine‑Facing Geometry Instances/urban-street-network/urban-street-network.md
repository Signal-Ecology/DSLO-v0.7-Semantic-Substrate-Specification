# Urban Street Network — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Urban street networks form routing geometries shaped by grids, radial hubs,
arterial hierarchies, and congestion gradients. Intersections, blocks, and
traffic signals create a multi‑scale topology that governs movement and flow.

**Key Properties**
- Grid or radial coherence  
- Hierarchical routing  
- Congestion gradients  
- Constraint‑bounded street formation  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: partitioning, propagation, routing  
- Invariants: grid/hub coherence, hierarchical stability  
- Manifolds: intersection, block‑geometry, routing‑hierarchy  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
