# Navajo Weaving Geometry — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Navajo weaving geometry arises from warp tension, weft insertion, color
sequencing, and recursive motifs. The loom defines boundaries, while the weaver
creates stable geometric patterns through embodied practice.

**Key Properties**
- Warp tension coherence  
- Recursive motifs (diamonds, steps)  
- Color‑sequence stability  
- Boundary‑anchored geometry  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: boundary formation, recursive growth, categorical encoding  
- Invariants: tension stability, motif coherence  
- Manifolds: warp, weft, motif
