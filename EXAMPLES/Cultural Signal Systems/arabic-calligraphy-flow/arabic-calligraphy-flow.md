# Arabic Calligraphy Flow — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Arabic calligraphy flow is a geometric system built from stroke curvature,
proportional units, and rhythmic sequencing. Flow emerges from pen angle,
pressure, and cultural training, producing stable geometry across scripts.

**Key Properties**
- Curvature coherence  
- Dot‑unit proportionality  
- Rhythmic stroke sequences  
- Multi‑scale flow geometry  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: orientation, rhythmic encoding, distributed cognition  
- Invariants: curvature families, proportional systems  
- Manifolds: curvature, stroke‑flow, proportional geometry  
- Dynamics: stylistic drift, collapse/restoration
