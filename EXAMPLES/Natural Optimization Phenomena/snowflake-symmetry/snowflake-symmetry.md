# Snowflake Symmetry — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Snowflakes form hexagonal, fractal patterns because ice’s lattice structure
forces growth along six permitted directions. Temperature and humidity gradients
drive branching, producing diffusion-limited crystalline geometry.

**Key Properties**
- Hexagonal lattice symmetry  
- Gradient-driven branching  
- Diffusion-limited growth  
- Multi-scale fractal structure  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: gradient, distributed alignment, optimization  
- Invariants: symmetry coherence, branching stability  
- Manifolds: hexagonal-symmetry, diffusion-gradient, branching-curvature  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
