## Overview
Magnetic field lines are geometric expressions of energy minimization and
gradient-aligned flow. They emerge from distributed interactions among moving
charges, dipoles, and boundary constraints. Field lines represent the direction
of least-energy curvature in the magnetic field and form coherent manifolds
around magnets, currents, and planetary bodies.

The geometry is continuous, smooth, and constraint-bounded: field lines never
intersect, align with gradient flow, and distribute curvature to minimize
magnetic energy.
