# Magnetic Field Lines — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Magnetic field lines form gradient-aligned curves that minimize magnetic energy.
They emerge from flux density, current distribution, and dipole orientation.
Field lines never intersect and create smooth, coherent manifolds.

**Key Properties**
- Gradient-aligned curvature  
- Energy-minimizing routing  
- Non-intersecting geometry  
- Multi-scale field coherence  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: gradient, distributed alignment, optimization  
- Invariants: curvature coherence, non-intersection stability  
- Manifolds: curvature-minimizing, gradient-flow, dipole-alignment  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
