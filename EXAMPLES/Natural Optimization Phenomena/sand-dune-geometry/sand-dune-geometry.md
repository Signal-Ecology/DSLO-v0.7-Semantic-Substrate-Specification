# Sand Dune Geometry — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Sand dunes form when wind gradients move grains up windward slopes and down
slip faces, creating curvature-minimizing surfaces. Dunes migrate, split, and
stabilize according to grain transport and shear stress.

**Key Properties**
- Wind-gradient alignment  
- Slip-face curvature  
- Grain-transport optimization  
- Crest migration  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: gradient, distributed load, optimization  
- Invariants: curvature coherence, slip-face stability  
- Manifolds: curvature-minimizing, wind-gradient, slip-face  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration  

This instance is part of the DSLO Natural Geometry Registry.
