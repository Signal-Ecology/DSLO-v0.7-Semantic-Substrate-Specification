## Overview
Sand dune geometry emerges from wind-flow gradients, grain transport, and
substrate constraints. Dunes form when grains accumulate on windward slopes and
cascade down slip faces, producing curvature-minimizing surfaces aligned with
prevailing wind direction.

Dunes migrate, split, merge, and stabilize according to optimization pressures:
minimizing shear stress, distributing grain load, and maintaining slip-face
coherence. Their geometry is a dynamic wave manifold shaped by fluid-grain
interaction.
