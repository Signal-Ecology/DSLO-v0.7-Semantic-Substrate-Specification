# River Delta Branching — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

River deltas form branching networks that distribute flow, minimize energy, and
balance sediment deposition. Channels split along hydraulic gradients, creating
a dynamic fractal geometry.

**Key Properties**
- Gradient-driven branching  
- Flow-distribution optimization  
- Sediment-bounded routing  
- Multi-scale fractal networks  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: gradient, distributed alignment, optimization  
- Invariants: branching coherence, routing stability
