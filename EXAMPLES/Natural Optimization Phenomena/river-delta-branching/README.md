## Overview
River delta branching is a flow-optimization geometry shaped by water velocity,
sediment load, and boundary constraints. Channels split when distributing flow
reduces energy expenditure, minimizes erosion, and stabilizes sediment
deposition. The resulting geometry is a multi-scale branching network aligned
with hydraulic gradients.

Deltas are dynamic fractals: channels form, collapse, and re-route as gradients
shift. The geometry emerges from distributed interactions among flow, sediment,
and substrate.
