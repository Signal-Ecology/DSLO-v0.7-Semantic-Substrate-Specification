# Crystal Growth Patterns — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Crystals grow by minimizing energy: atoms arrange into lattice structures,
growth fronts follow gradients, and facets emerge from symmetry constraints.
Diffusion, temperature, and boundary conditions shape the final geometry.

**Key Properties**
- Energy-minimizing lattice formation  
- Gradient-driven growth fronts  
- Faceting and symmetry  
- Branching under diffusion limits  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: gradient, distributed alignment, optimization  
- Invariants: lattice coherence, facet stability  
- Manifolds: lattice, facet-curvature, diffusion-gradient  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
