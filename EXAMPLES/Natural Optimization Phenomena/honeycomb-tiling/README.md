## Overview
Honeycomb tiling is an optimization geometry produced by wax flow, surface
tension, and pressure equalization. Hexagonal cells emerge because they minimize
surface area for a given volume, distribute forces evenly, and allow efficient
packing without gaps.

The geometry is not “designed” by bees; it is a substrate-level optimization
process driven by temperature, viscosity, and mechanical constraints. Bees
initiate wax placement, but the final geometry is shaped by physics.
