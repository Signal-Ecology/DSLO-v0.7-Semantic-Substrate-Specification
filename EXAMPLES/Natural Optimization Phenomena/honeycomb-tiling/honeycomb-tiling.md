# Honeycomb Tiling — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Honeycomb tiling emerges from physical optimization: wax flows, pressure
equalizes, and surface tension drives hexagonal packing. Bees start the process,
but physics finishes it.

**Key Properties**
- Surface-area minimization  
- Pressure-equalized geometry  
- Hexagonal symmetry  
- Gap-free packing  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: gradient, distributed equalization, optimization  
- Invariants: hexagonal coherence, curvature stability  
- Manifolds: tiling, surface-tension, pressure-distribution  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
