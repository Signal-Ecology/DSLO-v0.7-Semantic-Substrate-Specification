# Minimal Surface Geometry — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Minimal surfaces minimize area under boundary constraints. They have zero mean
curvature and appear in soap films, membranes, and energy‑minimizing systems.

**Key Properties**
- Zero mean curvature  
- Area minimization  
- Smooth equilibrium geometry  
- Curvature‑bounded manifolds  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: optimization, boundary curvature, equilibrium  
- Invariants: minimal area, stable curvature  
- Manifolds: minimal surface, boundary constraint  
- Dynamics: relaxation drift, collapse/restoration
