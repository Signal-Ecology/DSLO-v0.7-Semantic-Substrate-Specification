# L-Systems — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

L‑systems use simple rewriting rules to generate complex branching and fractal
geometry. Each iteration applies the same rules, producing self‑similar patterns
seen in plants, coral, and generative graphics.

**Key Properties**
- Recursive rewriting  
- Self‑similar geometry  
- Branching coherence  
- Deterministic growth  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: recursive growth, propagation, orientation  
- Invariants: recursion, branching coherence  
- Manifolds: rewriting, branching, orientation  
- Dynamics: iterative drift, collapse/restoration  

This instance is part of the DSLO Natural Geometry Registry.
