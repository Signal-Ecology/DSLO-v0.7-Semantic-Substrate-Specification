# Turing Patterns — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Turing patterns form when two chemicals react and diffuse at different rates,
creating stable spatial patterns such as stripes, spots, and spirals. They arise
from distributed interactions without centralized control.

**Key Properties**
- Reaction–diffusion geometry  
- Spontaneous pattern formation  
- Symmetry breaking  
- Stable spatial coherence  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: diffusion, activation–inhibition, equilibrium  
- Invariants: symmetry breaking, spatial coherence  
- Manifolds: reaction–diffusion, symmetry-breaking  
- Dynamics: stabilization drift, collapse/restoration
