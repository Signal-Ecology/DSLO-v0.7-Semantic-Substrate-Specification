# Coral Growth Geometry — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Coral colonies grow through distributed polyp behavior guided by nutrients,
water flow, and light. Branching, curvature, and colony shape emerge from
localized deposition decisions.

**Key Properties**
- Branching expansion  
- Flow‑aligned curvature  
- Gradient‑bounded growth  
- Distributed polyp decisions  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: gradient formation, recursive growth, orientation  
- Invariants: branching coherence, gradient stability  
- Manifolds: branching, gradient, curvature  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
