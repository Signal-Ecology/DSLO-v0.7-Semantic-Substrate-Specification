# Bee Waggle Dance — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Bees communicate direction, distance, and resource quality through a waggle
dance. Angle shows direction, waggle duration shows distance, and vibrational
strength shows quality. Other bees decode these signals through touch and
vibration.

**Key Properties**
- Angle → direction  
- Duration → distance  
- Vibration → quality  
- Stable collective decoding  

**DSLO Interpretation**
- Operators: orientation, gradient metric, rhythmic encoding  
- Invariants: angle‑duration coherence, vibrational stability  
- Manifolds: angular, duration, vibrational  
- Dynamics: drift, collapse/restoration
