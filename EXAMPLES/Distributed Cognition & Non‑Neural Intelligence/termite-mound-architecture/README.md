## Overview
Termite mounds are large‑scale architectural geometries generated through
distributed non‑neural cognition. Individual termites respond to local signals—
temperature, CO₂ concentration, humidity, air pressure, pheromone gradients—and
deposit soil accordingly. No termite understands the global structure; the mound
emerges from stigmergic coordination.

The mound acts as a living HVAC system: convection shafts, porous walls,
pressure‑regulated vents, and branching tunnels maintain stable internal
conditions despite external fluctuations. This is decentralized architectural
computation.
