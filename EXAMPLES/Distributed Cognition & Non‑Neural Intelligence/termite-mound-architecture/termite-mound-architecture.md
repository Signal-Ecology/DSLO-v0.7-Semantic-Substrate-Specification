# Termite Mound Architecture — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Termite mounds regulate temperature and airflow using decentralized construction.
Termites respond to CO₂, humidity, and pressure signals, building convection
shafts, porous walls, and branching tunnels that act as a natural HVAC system.

**Key Properties**
- Convection ventilation  
- Pressure‑regulated openings  
- Gradient‑bounded branching  
- Collective repair
