serrated edges, leaf-like tails, and substrate-matched coloration. Their geometry
is not merely visual; it is structural. Edge disruption, curvature alignment, and
texture mimicry combine to erase the gecko’s outline against foliage.

and environmental selection rather than real-time neural control. The gecko’s
