# Leaf-Tailed Gecko — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Leaf-tailed geckos mimic leaves using serrated edges, flattened bodies, leaf-like
tails, and substrate-matched coloration. Their geometry disrupts outlines and
aligns with foliage curvature and texture.

**Key Properties**
