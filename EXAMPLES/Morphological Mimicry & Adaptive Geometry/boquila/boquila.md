# Boquila — Summary (DSLO v0.7)

Boquila is a vine that mimics the leaves of nearby plants. It adapts leaf shape,
color, and venation using environmental cues such as light, chemicals, and
contact. Growth tips make local decisions that produce host‑matching geometry.

**Key Properties**
- Leaf‑shape mimicry  
- Gradient‑bounded adaptation  
- Distributed growth decisions  
- Multi‑feature morphological alignment  

**DSLO Interpretation**
